Dig

The unglamorous half

Yangbaga XL stainless steel litter box

Steel does not scratch up or hold odor the way plastic does, so it will not need replacing in two years. Extra large matters more than it sounds: cats want room to turn around, and a box that feels cramped is the usual reason one of them starts going next to it.

Pieviev double-layer litter mat

The top layer catches litter off their paws and it falls through to the bottom layer, so you tip it back into the box instead of vacuuming twice a day. One mat per box, right at the exit.

How many, and where

The rule is one box per cat plus one, so three for two kittens. Put them in different rooms rather than lined up in a row, and keep them away from the food. Most litter box problems are really placement problems.
